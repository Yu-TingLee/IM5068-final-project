# Love Taiwan (樂福推薦)

A referral platform for physical stores: customers share a store's referral code with friends, friends claim a coupon and redeem it in the store, and the referrer is rewarded once that redemption happens.

## People

**Customer** (顧客):
A person using the app, reached through the store's LINE official account.
_Avoid_: User, Member, Guest

**Partner** (推薦夥伴):
A Customer who has redeemed a Coupon at a Store and so holds that Store's Referral Code.
_Avoid_: Ambassador, Promoter

**Referrer** (推薦人):
The Partner whose Referral Code was used in a Referral.
_Avoid_: Inviter

**Referee** (被推薦人):
The Customer who claimed a coupon with someone else's Referral Code.
_Avoid_: Friend, New customer

## Stores and charity

**Store** (店家):
A physical business that pays only when a coupon is redeemed.
_Avoid_: Merchant, Shop

**Store Admin** (店家管理者):
A Store's staff member who signs into that Store's dashboard to see its Redemptions and fees.
_Avoid_: Owner, Merchant user

**Setup Code** (設定碼):
A one-time code the operator gives a new Store so it can choose its own dashboard password.
_Avoid_: Invite code, Temporary password

**Counter Code** (櫃台驗證碼):
A six-digit code shown at a Store's counter that changes every 10 minutes; a Customer enters it to prove they are in the Store.
_Avoid_: PIN, Store password, OTP

**Charity** (公益受贈機構):
The nonprofit a Store names to receive its Charity Contributions.
_Avoid_: Org, Beneficiary

## Referrals

**Referral Code** (推薦碼):
A Partner's code for one Store, written as `<store name>/<partner name>`.
_Avoid_: Promo code, Invite code

**Short Code** (短碼):
The compact form of a Referral Code, such as `LF-7K3M9Q`.

**Referral** (推薦):
The record of a Referee claiming a Referral Coupon with a Referrer's Referral Code.
_Avoid_: Invite

**Share Card** (分享卡):
The Partner's shareable view of their Referral Code, Short Code and claim link.

## Coupons

**Coupon** (折價券):
A discount owned by one Customer at one Store.
_Avoid_: Coupon Issue, Voucher, Ticket

**Welcome Coupon** (見面禮券):
The Coupon a Customer receives by entering a Store's Counter Code on their first visit.
_Avoid_: Counter coupon

**Referral Coupon** (推薦折價券):
The Coupon a Referee receives by entering a Referral Code.
_Avoid_: Referred coupon

**Reward Coupon** (推薦回饋券):
The Coupon a Referrer receives when their Referee redeems a Referral Coupon.
_Avoid_: Referrer reward

**Wallet** (優惠券匣):
The collection of Coupons a Customer owns.
_Avoid_: Coupon box

**Redemption** (核銷):
The act of using a Coupon at its Store, confirmed by that Store's Counter Code.
_Avoid_: Write-off, Use, Verify

## Money

**Platform Fee** (服務費):
The share of a redeemed Coupon's value the Store pays the platform.

**Charity Contribution** (公益提撥):
The share of a redeemed Coupon's value set aside for a Charity.
_Avoid_: Charity Fee, Donation

**Hearts** (愛心):
A Customer's running tally of charitable impact from their Redemptions.
_Avoid_: Love Hearts, Points
